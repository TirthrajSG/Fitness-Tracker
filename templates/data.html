Backup

There's no whole-database backup/restore here — with multiple accounts sharing one local database file, a raw file download would include every account's data, not just yours. Use the CSV export above as your personal backup. If you want a full copy of the database file for disaster recovery, copy instance/fitness.db directly from the project folder while the app isn't running.

Clear all data

Permanently deletes your weight entries, measurements, foods, food logs, exercises, workouts, and templates. Your account and settings are kept. This cannot be undone. Other accounts are unaffected.
